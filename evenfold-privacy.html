Evenfold Privacy Policy

Evenfold: Binary Logic Puzzle, by MofDevApp. Effective 5 November 2026.

Evenfold is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your settings, statistics, streak and puzzles in progress are saved only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Evenfold is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • general device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app version and IP address),
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped).

This data is sent encrypted to Google and its advertising partners and is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings under Google, Ads.

Children

Evenfold is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
